Buy and Sell Safely

🛒 If you are buying

  • Meet in a busy public place, in daytime if you can.
  • Check the item well and test it before you pay.
  • Never send money before you see the item.
  • Be careful with prices that look too good to be true.
  • Bring a friend for expensive items like phones and cars.
  • For cars, check the logbook and ID of the owner.

💰 If you are selling

  • Use clear, real photos of your own item.
  • Write an honest title, price and description.
  • Never share your PIN or passwords with anyone.
  • Confirm the money is in your account before handing over the item.
  • Ignore buyers who send fake M-Pesa messages or overpay and ask for change.
  • Delete your ad (My Dashboard) once the item is sold.
🚩 See something suspicious? Press Report on the ad and we will check it.

Not Allowed on SokoFlash

  1. Weapons, ammunition and explosives
  2. Illegal drugs and drug equipment
  3. Stolen goods or items without a legal owner
  4. Fake, counterfeit or copied products and fake documents
  5. Adult or sexual content and services
  6. Human or animal trafficking, and endangered animals or their parts
  7. Scams, pyramid schemes, "send money to get money" offers
  8. Prescription drugs without a licence, and dangerous chemicals
  9. Insults, hate, threats or spam in comments
  10. Anything else that breaks Kenyan law

Ads and comments that break these rules are removed. Accounts can be restricted or banned.

FAQ

How much does it cost to post an ad?Posting costs KES and your ad stays live for days.
What is Boost?Normal ads show in order of newest. A boosted ad is shown at the top, with a 🚀 badge, so more people see it. A boost lasts days and costs KES . You can boost when you post or later from My Dashboard.
How do I pay?A secure Paystack window opens. Pay with M-Pesa or card. We confirm your payment and your ad goes live (or gets boosted).
How do buyers contact me?Through WhatsApp, using the phone number you registered with. Make sure it is a number you use on WhatsApp.
Can people see who viewed my ad?Yes. In My Dashboard you can see the names of logged-in people who viewed your ad, and your total views, likes and followers.
How do followers work?Open a seller's profile and press Follow. The seller sees how many followers they have.
I paid but my ad is not liveWait a little while we confirm. If it takes long, contact support on WhatsApp and send your Paystack reference.
Why was my account restricted or banned?Breaking the rules, scams, abuse or spam. Contact support if you think it was a mistake.
What happens when my ad expires?It stops showing. Post a new ad to sell again.
Does SokoFlash handle delivery of items?No. We only take the fee for posting or boosting. Buyers and sellers agree on payment and meeting directly. Stay safe.
